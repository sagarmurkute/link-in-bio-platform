# VYB Product Vision

## Executive Summary

VYB is an account-based personal identity and link-in-bio platform built to unify fragmented online presence under one custom URL (`getvyb.vercel.app/username`).

## Core Philosophy

> **One identity. One place. One link.**

In a world where creators, developers, designers, and professionals are scattered across 10+ platforms (Instagram, GitHub, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, portfolios, stores), VYB provides a quiet, intentional, highly-customizable single URL representing their complete digital footprint.

## Primary Capabilities

1. **Custom Handle Ownership**: Instant handle reservation and ownership (`getvyb.vercel.app/handle`).
2. **Link & Content Stack**: Drag-and-drop link stack creation powered by `@dnd-kit`.
3. **Themes & Aesthetics**: High-energy, Linktree-inspired colors (Electric Lime `#D2E823`, Deep Pine `#254F1A`, Electric Purple `#7800FF`, Magenta `#FF1493`).
4. **Real-time Analytics**: Link click and profile view analytics.
